The problem

Important details get buried in busy conversations.

When someone returns to a group chat, they may have to scroll through dozens or hundreds of messages just to find out what changed and what needs their attention.

↗

Too much to scan

✓

Tasks are easy to miss

Requests, owners, and due dates can disappear in the thread.

◇

Decisions lose context

It is hard to distinguish a final decision from a passing suggestion.

⌁

Privacy matters

Work or personal conversations should not need to leave the device for a basic recap.

The solution

One calm workspace for your catch-up.

Paste a transcript or import a supported text, CSV, or JSON export. Lucent analyzes it in the browser and organizes useful signals into a short brief and reviewable views.

✦

A quick orientation to likely decisions, actions, and timing signals.

☑

Action board + timeline

Edit task details, track progress, and review upcoming timing.

⇩

Useful outputs

Export a Markdown brief, or create calendar entries from dates you explicitly confirm.

Lucent helps you find what to look at next. It is a catch-up assistant—not a replacement for the original conversation.

Advantages and responsible use

Fast orientation, with the human still in control.

Why Lucent is useful

  • ✓Reduces the effort of manually scanning a long transcript.
  • ✓Keeps ordinary rule-based analysis in the browser without a hosted AI service.
  • ✓Lets you edit, review, and choose what to save or export.
  • ✓Supports follow-through comparisons and local search for saved chats.

Use it effectively

  • ✓Include speaker names and timestamps when available.
  • ✓Check each suggested action against its original message and surrounding context.
  • !Rule-based signals can miss nuance or flag a message incorrectly.
  • !Optional browser AI depends on browser/device support and may require a model download; verify its suggestions too.
  • !Only confirm dates you have checked. Local saved chats stay in this browser profile until you delete them.
